# Wheels Up

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md)

The [Wheels Up](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wheels-up/id956615077) app is a trusted private aviation solution for individuals, families and businesses. Whether you're booking your own flight or looking to share with other members, the app makes it easy to search and book the aircraft option that meets your financial and travel needs.

**Focus:** Flight search, booking logistics, member sharing

## Details
* **Technologies:** Swift, MVC
* **Platform:** iOS, iPhone
* **App Store:** [Wheels Up](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wheels-up/id956615077)
